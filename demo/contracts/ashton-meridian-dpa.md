DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT
Contract Ref: DPA-2025-014

This Data Processing Agreement is made as of 1 January 2025 between
Ashton Medical Devices, Inc. ("Controller") and Meridian Cloud Services Ltd.
("Processor"), and supplements Master Services Agreement Ref: MSA-2025-014.

1. SCOPE
   1.1 The Processor processes personal data only on the documented instructions
       of the Controller.

4. SECURITY
   4.1 The Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organisational
       measures to protect personal data.

5. RETURN AND DELETION
   5.1 On termination or expiry of the services, the Processor shall, at the
       Controller's election, delete or return all personal data processed on
       the Controller's behalf, and delete existing copies, within thirty (30)
       days of such termination, save to the extent retention is required by law.
   5.2 The Processor shall certify completion of such deletion or return in
       writing on request.

7. SUB-PROCESSORS
   7.1 The Processor shall not engage a sub-processor without the Controller's
       prior written authorisation.
