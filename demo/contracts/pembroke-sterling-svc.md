REGULATORY REPORTING SERVICES AGREEMENT
Contract Ref: SVC-2025-021

This Regulatory Reporting Services Agreement is made as of 1 July 2025 between
Pembroke Ridge Capital, L.P. ("Client") and Sterling Compliance Advisors LLP
("Provider").

1. SERVICES
   1.1 The Provider shall provide regulatory reporting and compliance support
       services to the Client.

2. TERM
   2.1 This Agreement continues for a term commencing on the Effective Date and
       ending 30 June 2027, unless terminated earlier in accordance with its
       terms.

4. CLIENT OBLIGATIONS
   4.1 The Client shall provide the Provider with the information reasonably
       required to perform the services.
   4.2 The Client shall deliver to the Provider a compliance attestation, in the
       form set out in Schedule 2, on a quarterly basis, within fifteen (15) days
       after the end of each calendar quarter.

6. FEES
   6.1 The Client shall pay the Provider the fees set out in Schedule 1.

EXECUTED by the parties.
