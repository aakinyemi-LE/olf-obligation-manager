MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT
Contract Ref: MSA-2025-014

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is made and entered into as of
1 January 2025 (the "Effective Date")

BETWEEN:
  Ashton Medical Devices, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Customer"); and
  Meridian Cloud Services Ltd., a company incorporated in England ("Supplier").

RECITALS
The Supplier provides cloud hosting and managed platform services. The Customer
wishes to procure those services on the terms set out below.

1. SERVICES
   1.1 The Supplier shall provide the services described in each Order Form and
       any Statement of Work executed under this Agreement.

6. FEES AND PRICE ADJUSTMENT
   6.1 The Customer shall pay the fees set out in the applicable Order Form.
   6.4 The Supplier may increase the fees with effect from any renewal term by
       no more than the increase in the Consumer Price Index plus three per cent
       (CPI + 3%), provided that the Supplier gives the Customer not less than
       forty-five (45) days' prior written notice of any such increase.

11. TERM AND RENEWAL
   11.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an
        initial term ending 31 December 2026 (the "Initial Term").
   11.2 On expiry of the Initial Term this Agreement shall automatically renew
        for successive periods of twelve (12) months (each a "Renewal Term")
        unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal not less than
        sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term.

14. INSURANCE
   14.3 Throughout the term the Supplier shall maintain cyber-liability insurance
        of not less than US$10,000,000 per occurrence and shall furnish the
        Customer with a certificate of insurance evidencing such cover on an
        annual basis.

18. DATA PROTECTION
   18.1 The parties have entered into a separate Data Processing Agreement
        (Ref: DPA-2025-014), which forms part of this Agreement.
